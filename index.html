Filter options
  • Explore character references. Choose a family or search for a character. Use Review images to inspect existing references, or Needs images to see missing types and prepare a request for chat.
  • Approve individual images. Use Approve all in a category to approve its images. Approvals apply to the exact image version; your selected reference stays the same. Ranking or preferring an image does not approve it.
  • Rank your references. Open Rank / reorder category, then use the position menu, drag handles, or move buttons. Ranks stay as they are until you reorder them.
  • Set aside old options. Mark an image obsolete to remove it from current choices. You can bring it back later.
  • Save and share your decisions. Changes save automatically on this Mac. Tell the assistant to read your latest choices when you’re ready—it isn’t notified by a save.
  • Find other roster entries. Missing roles stay listed. Use Roster scope to see earlier drafts or mentioned-only figures.
  • Using an offline file? Export your choices to hand them off.
