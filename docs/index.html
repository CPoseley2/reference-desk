01 / ACCOUNTS

Do I need an AI account?

No account is needed to organize, compare, and approve existing images. Optional image generation needs your own OpenAI API key. Generated images remain candidates until explicitly approved.

02 / THE APP

Where does it run?

The workspace runs locally on your Mac and opens in your browser. An optional native wrapper can be built with Xcode command-line tools. This website introduces the software; it does not host the app or your libraries.

03 / YOUR CONTENT

Are references included?

Your first launch creates an empty library. No production inventory or private reference imagery is bundled. The artwork here illustrates a fictional production. Content you add retains its own ownership and usage rights.

04 / BACKUPS

Keep a copy of your work.

Stop the app and copy the complete working folder, including libraries, Command Center runtime folders, and hidden .state. Git ignores private runtime data. Linked sources and original media also need their own backups.
